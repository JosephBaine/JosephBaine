Joseph Baine

Postdoctoral researcher · Max-Planck Institute for Mathematics

I study Hecke categories and am motivated by questions arising from geometric representation theory, tensor categories and algebraic combinatorics.

I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the Max-Planck Institute for Mathematics under the mentorship of Dennis Gaitsgory. Previously, I completed a postdoctoral fellowship at the University of New South Wales with Daniel Chan. Before that I completed my PhD at the University of Sydney with Geordie Williamson.

Email: baine@mpim-bonn.mpg.de
